Lesson 8 - Nmap
Date: 20 Nov 2026

Nmap = kayan duba abin da ke kan network
127.0.0.1 = computer na kaina (localhost)
-sn = duba waye ke kunne kawai
-sV = duba service da version

Abin da na yi:
Na yi nmap -sn 192.168.1.0/24 - na samu 0 hosts, saboda ba network na bane
Na yi nmap -sn 192.168.185.0/24 - na samu 2 hosts:
  192.168.185.91 = gateway / router
  192.168.185.142 = computer na
Na yi nmap 127.0.0.1 - na samu ports 2 a bude:
  631/tcp = ipp (printer)
  3389/tcp = ms-wbt-server (remote desktop)
  998 ports a rufe suke - computer na yana da tsaro

Darasi: Kada ayi scan na network na wani ba tare da izini ba
Yi amfani da Nmap don kare network dinka kawai
